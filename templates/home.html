Bio
I am a first-year in the Schreyer Honors College at the Pennsylvania State University majoring in chemical engineering and plant sciences with hopeful minors in creative writing and biochemistry. I have particular interests in learning and architecting the chemical systems of plants: in their internal biochemistry, their chemical ecological interactions, and in the application of those processes in service to society.

A cross-country coach once told me on picking when to run vs. jog during races that I ought to sprint up the hills, and slow going down them. My approach towards academics is well summarized by that strategy: quality, thoroughness, and challenging myself in the interest of growth are all fundamentals. I am in university to take lots of classes, to struggle, and to end up not just more knowledgeable, but a stronger student than when I started.

Outside of academics, I am a member and the current art editor for Kalliope, a novice rower for Club Crew, and am working to be able to identify the 500 most common plants in the forests, fields, and bogs of Central Pennsylvania. I am excited to be helping with research this summer between the grain production and weed science labs at Penn State, where I hope to translate the work I've done this year into research skills in the agricultural sciences.
